# 《治驗録》〈兵庫縣病院入院患者表〉（1873–1874 年住院紀錄，神戶大學 0100399104） / Chikenroku [Hyōgo Prefectural Hospital Inpatient Register]: Transcription Dataset

〈兵庫縣病院入院患者表〉刊在美國宣教醫約翰別麗（John Cutting Berry）的《治驗録》（1874）。表中逐列記錄自明治六年七月七日至七年七月十八日的住院病例，包括年齡、職業、居處、入院病症、分等與救助、出院日期、滯院日數及轉歸。底本為日本神戶大學附屬圖書館醫學分館藏 0100399104。可供明治初年醫院、住院照護、醫療救助史與宣教醫學研究。

The Hyōgo Prefectural Hospital Inpatient Register comes from Chikenroku (1874) by the American medical missionary John Cutting Berry. Its entries record age, occupation, place of residence, presenting condition, admission class and assistance, discharge date, duration of stay, and outcome for July 1873 to July 1874. The source is held by the Medical Library of Kobe University, record 0100399104. It supports research on early Meiji hospitals, inpatient care, medical assistance, and medical missions.

The complete titled table and its printed totals are preserved in 12 selected scans from the left side of image 11 through the right side of image 23. TXT, CSV, JSON, and TEI XML share the source locations. AI-assisted transcription of the printed cells, including uncertain and empty entries. Human collation and expert review are pending.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。採早期明治文獻的漢字表述，少數片假名照錄。保留覆指「同」、印刷原日期、格內雙欄、原空白、轉歸欄用字。原刊統計和逐格資料各自保存。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. The original kanji and occasional katakana are preserved, including ditto marks, literal dates, split cells, blank fields, and outcome labels. Printed totals are represented separately from transcribed entries. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
